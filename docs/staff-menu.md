# Changing the food menu

The food menu on the website is edited here:

https://michaelsvillagepub.net/edit

There is no separate account. The password box on that page is the login. Ask the owner for the menu password.

## Sign in

1. Open https://michaelsvillagepub.net/edit
2. Type the menu password.
3. Click **Log in**. The menu stays hidden until the password is correct.
4. The browser remembers that password until you close it. Type it again on the next visit.

## Change a dish

1. Find the section, such as Starters or Salads.
2. Edit the Name or the Detail.
3. Click **Save menu** at the bottom.
4. Wait until the page says **Saved to MongoDB.**
5. Open the home page and the takeout page, then refresh. Both menus should show the new wording. The takeout page leaves the photos off.

## Add a dish

1. In the right section, click **Add item**.
2. Replace “New item” with the dish name.
3. Write the description in Detail.
4. Click **Save menu**.

## Remove a dish

1. Click **Remove** under that dish.
2. Click **Save menu**. Nothing is deleted until you save.

## Add a section

1. Click **Add section**.
2. Name the section.
3. Click **Add item** for each dish in it.
4. Click **Save menu**.

A whole section cannot be deleted from this page. Remove its dishes, or ask Shinddo Software to delete the section.

## Photos

Add a photo only when that dish should appear in the gallery.

1. Click **Upload photo**.
2. Choose a JPG, PNG, or WebP. The file must be under 5 MB.
3. Click **Save menu**. The photo is not kept on the dish until you save.

To take a picture off the site, click **Remove photo**, then **Save menu**.

## What this page does not change

- Early Bird and Lunch specials
- Hours, phone number, and address
- Gift cards

Guests do not see dish prices on the dining menu or the takeout menu. The Price box is kept for your records. Dollar amounts typed into Detail are also left off the public menu. Photos stay off the takeout page.

## If it does not save

- **Wrong password.** The password is incorrect, and nothing was saved.
- **Save failed.** or **The menu didn’t load.** Try again. If it keeps happening, contact Shinddo Software.
- The success message is **Saved to MongoDB.**
