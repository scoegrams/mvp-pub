# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated, then constrained by the host the user named: a static site served by a small Node process so Railway has a start command. The user did not pick a framework.

## Users

Inferred (the product interview was interrupted; the user answered hosting only): people in and around Plymouth who are deciding whether to come to a neighborhood pub for food and a game, usually on a phone.

## Product Purpose

A public website for Michael’s Village Pub so a visitor can recognize the place, know where it is, and decide to come in. Success is a first-time visitor who can name the pub, place it on State Road in Plymouth, and take the next step toward showing up.

## Positioning

The village sports pub on State Road in Plymouth, known by the MVP crest: food and a game in one room. A generic “American restaurant” template could not carry that mark.

## Operating Context

One address: 2294 State Rd, Plymouth, MA 02360. Visitors arrive from search, maps, and word of mouth. The site is hosted on Railway.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed: the pub name, the crest, the Plymouth address, and Railway hosting.

Undecided, and not to be invented as fact: phone number, hours, menu, prices, online ordering, reservations, private events, and which games are on.

The crest ribbon reads “GREAT FOOD + GOOD TIMES.” The user also wrote “GREAT FOOD GREAT TIMES.” Both stay on record until one is chosen as the public line.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Michael’s Village Pub
- Mark: `docs/mvp.png` (crest with beer, football, baseball, basketball, hockey)
- Spoken line from the user: Great Food Great Times
- Line printed on the crest: Great Food + Good Times

## Evidence on Hand

- Crest file: `docs/mvp.png`
- Address, given by the user: 2294 State Rd, Plymouth, MA 02360
- No verified hours, phone, menu, reviews, or photography. Future work must not present those as real.

## Product Principles

- The crest is the name people already recognize.
- One location, easy to reach from the page.
- A sports pub, not a fine-dining restaurant.
- Unverified commercial facts stay unmarked or absent.
